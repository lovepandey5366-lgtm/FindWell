How Campus Lost & Found Works

A structured 3-step lifecycle for quick item recovery

1. Report Details

Submit details, category, location, date, and optional photos. Choose your contact preference securely.

2. Smart Matching

Our automated algorithm cross-references lost reports with found items based on keyword analysis, category, and date proximity.

3. Claim & Handover

Submit an ownership claim with identifying proof. Once verified by the reporter or campus admin, arrange a safe handover.
